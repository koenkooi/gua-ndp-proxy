# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and
send patches without being treated badly for it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Give and accept feedback on technical grounds.
- Respect other people's time: keep reports reproducible and on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or sexualised attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break
these rules, and may block people who repeat the behaviour. To report a
problem, contact a maintainer privately, using the contact details on their
GitHub profile. Reports are treated confidentially.

This applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project in
public.
